Our Story

Founded in 2018 in the heart of Bole, Habesha Eatery brings authentic Ethiopian flavours in Addis Ababa. We use family recipes, fresh ingredients from local markets and cooking techniques that bring out the taste of our spices. Every dish tells a story of tradition, community and the warmth of Ethiopian hospitality.
